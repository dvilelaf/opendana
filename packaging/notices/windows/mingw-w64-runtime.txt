The MinGW-w64 cross linker copies a few members of the MinGW-w64 C runtime and
of libgcc into bin/opendana.exe and bin/SDL2.dll (static start-up code, printf/
strtod helpers and math functions). Neither executable imports a MinGW runtime
DLL (no libgcc_s, libwinpthread or libstdc++ DLL); the thread model is win32,
so winpthreads is not linked. The members were identified with linker maps
(-Wl,-Map) of both executables.

Linked members (union of both executables; linker maps -Wl,-Map) and the
notices that apply. Where the upstream licence of a member is not certain, every
notice that could apply is reproduced:

  libmingw32.a: charmax, cinitexe, crt0_c, CRT_fp10, crt_handler, dllargv,
    gccmain, merr, mingw_helpers, natstart, _newmode, pesect, pseudo-reloc,
    pseudo-reloc-list, tlsmcrt, tlsmthread, tlssup, tlsthrd, usermatherr,
    wildcard, xncommod, xtxtmode; crt2.o / dllcrt2.o  -> sections 1 and 2
  libmingwex.a: gdtoa, dmisc, gethex, gmisc, hd_init, hexnan, misc, smisc,
    strtodg, strtopx, sum                               -> section 3 (Lucent)
  libmingwex.a: strtodnrp                              -> section 4 (David M. Gay)
  libmingwex.a: strtok_r                               -> section 5 (BSD-3-Clause)
  libmingwex.a: mingw_pformat                          -> sections 3 and 6
  libmingwex.a: mingw_vfprintf, mingw_vfscanf, mingw_vsnprintf,
    mingw_vsprintf                                     -> sections 1 and 6
  libmingwex.a: math members (acosf, asinf, atan, atan2, atan2f, atanf, cos,
    cosf, cosl_internal, exp, exp2l, fmod, fmodf, internal_logl, ldexp, log,
    log10f, log2l, logf, lround, lroundf, mingw_matherr, pow, powi, round,
    roundf, scalbn, scalbnf, sin, sinf, sinl_internal, sqrt, sqrtf, tanf)
                                                       -> sections 1 and 7
  libmingwex.a: other helpers (dirent, _stat, dllentry, dllmain, fseeko64,
    ftello64, isblank, strnlen, strtof, strtoimax, strtoumax, wcsnlen)
                                                       -> section 1
  libgcc.a: _chkstk_ms, _ctors, _divdi3, _udivdi3; libgcc_eh.a: emutls
                                                       -> section 8
