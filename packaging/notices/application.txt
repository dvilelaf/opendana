This is the native reimplementation itself (the opendana executable). Its complete
corresponding source is sources/application.tar.xz in this bundle: the
`git archive` of the exact commit printed by `opendana --version` (the packaging
gate refuses a package whose source archive is of another commit). The version
and commit are also listed in SOURCES.json.

The full GNU General Public License version 3 text is the LICENSE file next
to README.md in this package (and in the source archive); the project's
intellectual-property statement is the NOTICE file.

The application is an unofficial, from-scratch reimplementation of the NES
game; the player imports their own legally owned ROM once. Every package is
checked with the project's ROM auditor before it is published (the audit
report travels next to the package).
